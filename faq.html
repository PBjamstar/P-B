Help

Questions & answers

Everything new members ask first

How do I join Phantom Bloodline?
Fill in the application on this site. Leadership reads every application and replies on Discord, so keep your DMs open.
What happens after I'm accepted?
New members start on trial. Rank is earned through loyalty, conduct and dedication, so activity and attitude decide how fast you move up. The full ladder is on the homepage.
Which server do you play on?
We are a Cops & Robbers crew on FiveM EU1.
How active do I need to be?
Stay active in Discord and in-game. Inactivity of 14 days or more without notice may result in removal, so tell staff if you are going to be away.
What are the crew rules?
The ten Laws of the Bloodline are on the homepage. In short: respect everyone, stay loyal, stay active, play fair, and represent the crew well. Leadership has the final say.
What does being blacklisted mean?
Blacklisted people are not eligible to apply and are listed on the Blacklist page.
I was blacklisted. Can I appeal?
Yes. Use the appeal form and be honest and detailed. Appeals are reviewed by leadership within 72 hours, and if accepted you are contacted on Discord.
Where do I find your latest news?
On the News page, and in the announcements channel on Discord.
Who runs the crew?
The staff list is on the homepage, and the Roster shows every member by rank.
I used to be in the crew. Where did I go?
Former members are kept on the Alumni page.
